Privacy policy

Arcline · last updated 18 October 2026

Arcline is a watch face for Wear OS. This policy explains what it does with your data: nothing leaves your watch.

What Arcline shows

Arcline displays information your watch already has: the time and date, your heart rate, the weather, and the complications you choose (such as steps or battery). This information is provided by Wear OS and the apps on your watch, and is only drawn on the screen.

What Arcline collects

Nothing. Arcline contains no code of its own (it is built with Google's Watch Face Format), makes no network connections, and does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics and no accounts.

Your settings

Your choices in the watch face editor (theme, complications and so on) are stored by Wear OS on your watch.

Third parties

Complications you choose come from other apps on your watch, which have their own privacy policies.
